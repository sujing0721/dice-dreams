About Dice Dreams Strategy Hub

Who we are

Dice Dreams Strategy Hub is a fan-run guide for Dice Dreams players. We are not a gaming company, a news outlet, or an official partner of SuperPlay. Everything here comes from players who have spent real time in the game and wanted to write down what actually works — so other players don't have to figure it out from scratch.

The guide exists because most free Dice Dreams advice online is either recycled from the same handful of videos, or buried inside pages whose real purpose is getting you to click a "free dice generator" link. We wanted a single text-based resource that covers the parts of the game that matter: dice economy, bet multiplier, attack and shield tactics, card and team play, and event timing — across a set of focused pages rather than one giant wall.

What's on this site

  • Free dice sources — the legitimate daily systems, plus a scam checklist
  • Bet multiplier strategy — when to raise the bet and when to drop it
  • Interactive calculators — a dice planner and a bet trade-off tool
  • Attack & defence — targeting, shield timing and protecting your pile
  • Castle build order — how to spend coins so you advance dreamlands
  • Cards & team — finishing sets through trading and using teams
  • Events guide — the recurring event types and how to time a reserve
  • Beginner tips & mistakes — the habits to build and the ones to drop

How we keep content current

Dice Dreams rotates its events constantly, so exact timings and reward values change week to week. We deliberately describe the types of events and the general strategy behind them rather than specific dates or point values, because those go stale fast — live timers and rewards always come from the app itself. Everything we publish follows the process on our editorial standards page: a tip is tested in a live account before it appears, and every guide carries a visible author line.

If you spot something that looks wrong or outdated, you're welcome to reach out via our contact page. Feedback from active players is what keeps the guide accurate.

What this site is not

We don't sell in-game currency, offer account boosting, or provide any "hack", "generator", or "cheat" tool. Any such service is against Dice Dreams' Terms of Service, usually a scam, and puts accounts at risk. All content here is strategy guidance only — you play the game normally, we just help you play it more deliberately. We also never promise real-money earnings; Dice Dreams is an entertainment game with a virtual economy.
