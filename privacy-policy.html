Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy explains how Dice Dreams Strategy Hub ("we", "us", "this site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

This site does not require registration or login. We do not ask you to submit personal information — including any game ID, friend code, or account details — to access any part of the guide. We never ask for your game credentials, and you should never enter them on this or any other guide site.

We may collect the following types of information automatically through standard web technologies:

  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent on each page, referring website, approximate geographic region, device type, and browser type. This data is aggregated and used to understand which sections of the guide are most useful.
  • Cookies: small text files placed on your device by this site and by third-party services we use (see below).

How we use information

  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve and expand the guide
  • To diagnose technical issues and maintain site performance
  • To serve relevant advertising through third-party ad networks

Our interactive tools

The dice planner and bet multiplier calculator on the calculator page run entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter stay on your device — they are not transmitted to us, not stored on a server, and not logged. Reloading the page resets them.

Cookies and tracking technologies

This site uses cookies set by itself and by third-party providers. Third-party providers include Google (for advertising and analytics) and potentially other ad-tech partners. Cookies are used for:

  • Remembering basic preferences (such as event-filter selections during your session)
  • Aggregated analytics to measure page performance
  • Ad targeting and frequency capping by our advertising partners

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from reading the guide, but may disable the search and calculator features.

Your consent (cookie notice)

When you first visit, we show a cookie notice asking you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. We respect your choice: advertising and analytics cookies are only enabled with your consent, and you can change your decision at any time by clearing your browser's stored data for this site or contacting us. Essential cookies that make the page work are always active.

Google AdSense

This site displays advertising served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.

  • Google's DART cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on this site and which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Other technologies (such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons) may also be used by third-party ad networks to measure the effectiveness of their advertising and/or to personalise the advertising content.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects standard log data and visitor behaviour information, processed to produce reports about website activity. No personally identifying information is collected by Google Analytics on this site. To learn more about how Google uses data, visit Google's Privacy & Terms page.

Third-party links

This site refers to external community resources, such as card trading groups. We do not control those websites or services and are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of each site you visit.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

Your rights (EEA and UK visitors)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, data-protection law gives you rights over your personal information — including the right to access it, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to or withdraw consent for processing such as advertising cookies. Because we collect no personal data directly and handle the rest through Google under the consent you give, exercising these rights is straightforward: make your choice in the cookie notice, adjust your browser settings, or email us via the contact page and we'll act on your request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example when we add new features or change our use of third-party services. When we make changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy or how we handle data, please reach out via our contact page.
