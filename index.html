Start Here — The Core Loop

Getting Started

Everything in Dice Dreams runs through one loop: you roll dice, dice give coins, coins upgrade your castle, and upgrading moves you to the next dreamland. Attacks, cards and events all hang off that loop. Understand the loop and every decision below starts to make sense.

Step 1

Roll — the bet sets your pace

Each roll costs dice equal to your current bet multiplier and returns coins scaled by that same bet. A low bet stretches a stack across many rolls (more chances to hit cards and triggers); a high bet drains it fast but pays out big coins per roll. This single trade-off drives almost every strategy on the site — the bet guide and calculator cover it in depth.

Step 2

Build — finish the castle, move on

Coins go into castle and landmark upgrades. Completing a level advances you to a new dreamland where every roll is worth more. Depth in one castle is almost always worth less than progress to the next — the build-order guide shows how to spend so you keep moving.

Step 3

Attack & collect — the side rewards

Landing on attack spaces lets you raid other players; landing on collection spaces adds cards to your team album. Cards complete sets for big payouts, and attacks are worth far more during boosted events. Start with the attack guide and the cards & team guide.

Step 4

Stack for events — where dice multiply

Timed events pay back multiples on rolls you would take anyway. The players who pull ahead hold a dice reserve and spend it inside a boosted window rather than trickling it in on a quiet day. The events guide explains which event types are worth saving for.

Quick Answers

FAQ

Three questions that come up constantly. The full set lives on the dedicated FAQ page.

Q · A

How do you get free dice?

Friend gifts, daily quests and chests, event milestone rewards, and team rewards are the steady, legitimate sources. None of them require a login, a download, or a survey — which is exactly how you tell them apart from the fake "generator" sites. The free dice page ranks them by what they actually pay.

Q · A

Is the game safe?

Yes — Dice Dreams is a normal free-to-play title from SuperPlay, distributed through the official app stores. The real things to watch are in-app spending pressure and third-party "free dice" websites, not the app itself. We cover the safety angle honestly on the what-is-dice-dreams page.

Q · A

Should I bet high or low?

High to collect coins for a castle jump; low to farm cards, chests and event triggers. Coins scale with the bet, but special triggers fire per roll — so a high bet gives you fewer of them. The calculator turns this into a concrete number for your stack.
