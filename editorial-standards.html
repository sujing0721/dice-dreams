How we work

Editorial Standards

Our one promise

Dice Dreams Strategy Hub publishes advice we actually use in our own accounts. No recycled video scripts, no fake "generator" clickbait, and no invented reward numbers. This page describes the process behind every guide on the site so you know how much to trust it.

How a tip gets published

  • Tested in a live account — a strategy only appears here after we have used it ourselves and watched it change results.
  • Re-checked at a different stage — we confirm it still holds early and late, not just in one dreamland where it happened to work.
  • Written from shared notes — every page comes from our own play notes, not from copying another site.
  • Described as archetypes, not dates — for events we explain the recurring type and the strategy around it, never specific dates or point values that go stale within a week.

How we keep pages current

Dice Dreams rotates events constantly, so exact timings and reward amounts change week to week. We review and update guides when major mechanics shift, and every guide carries a visible author line and a reviewed-and-updated date so you can judge how fresh the advice is. Live timers and reward values always come from the app itself — not from us.

Our stance on scams and real money

We never link to, endorse, or describe a "free dice generator", hack, cheat, or paid-currency service. Those violate the game's Terms of Service, and almost all of them exist to harvest logins or push fake apps. We also never promise that Dice Dreams pays real money — it is an entertainment game with a virtual economy, and any site claiming otherwise is a scam. Where a topic touches a risk (trading safety, spending pressure, generator pages), we say so plainly.

Advertising and independence

This site may display advertising served through third-party providers such as Google AdSense. Advertising never influences our editorial content: we do not accept payment in exchange for featuring a product, strategy, or third-party service, and ad placement is independent of what we write. Our interactive calculators run entirely in your browser and send nothing anywhere.

Corrections

If you think something on the site is wrong or out of date, tell us via the contact page. We verify reports against our own accounts and update where needed — player corrections are genuinely how this guide stays accurate.
